# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Security analysts (SOC Tier 1–2), security engineers, and CISOs at small-to-medium enterprises. Also potential employers and clients evaluating the platform and its builder. Users arrive with high technical literacy and a high skepticism bar — they read code, not brochures.

## Product Purpose
AegisTrace is a full-stack security operations platform built for identity-first threat detection and investigation. The public-facing pages serve two audiences: technical security buyers evaluating the platform, and recruiters/clients evaluating Prasanna Kumar as a builder. Success means a visitor leaves believing this was built by someone who knows exactly what they are doing.

## Brand Personality
Precise. Commanding. Cinematic. The brand does not shout; it states. Confidence comes from specificity, not superlatives.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS cream-and-midnight-blue (CrowdStrike look-alikes, generic SIEM vendors)
- Glowing-green hacker-movie aesthetic (Matrix green, neon, cyberpunk cliché)
- Template landing pages with icon-heading-text identical card grids
- Startup-optimism copy: "empower," "supercharge," "game-changer," "seamless"
- Constellation, particle, or grid-line backgrounds as the primary visual element
- Progress bars for skills (reductive and imprecise)

## Design Principles
1. Specificity over superlatives. Every claim is backed by a real number or a named feature, not an adjective.
2. The photograph IS the design. Background imagery carries the atmosphere; typography states the case.
3. Depth without decoration. 3D effects serve spatial hierarchy — not aesthetics for their own sake.
4. Each page is its own world. Landing, Portfolio, Login, and Mission have distinct visual atmospheres that share a typeface and accent color but not a layout template.
5. Motion earns its place. Animations serve entrance, feedback, or hierarchy — never pure decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA minimum. Body text at minimum 4.5:1 contrast on dark backgrounds. Large headings at minimum 3:1. prefers-reduced-motion: all animations reduce to instant crossfades. No content gated behind animation state.
