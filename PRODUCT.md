# AegisTrace — PRODUCT.md

## Register
brand

## Product
AegisTrace is a solo-built Identity Threat Detection & Response (ITDR) platform — a Trust Operating System for the AI-agent era. It replaces five disconnected security tools (SIEM, endpoint agent, identity graph, AI triage, compliance reports) with one unified SOC platform. Built by a practitioner who ran real incident pipelines with Microsoft Sentinel and found the gaps impossible to ignore.

## Target Users
- **SOC analysts** at small-to-mid teams who pivot between 5 tools per investigation
- **Security engineers** building detection on stacks not designed for identity threats
- **CISOs** evaluating modern identity-first detection for AI-era environments
- **Students and researchers** learning incident response with a real, deployable platform
- **Hiring managers / technical evaluators** assessing Prasanna Kumar Surendran's engineering depth

## Brand Personality
Precise. Vigilant. Uncompromising. Like a bespoke instrument, not a corporate suite.

Three words: **sharp, authoritative, alive**.

## Emotional Goal
Visitors should feel: "This is serious. This person knows exactly what they're doing. I want this."
Not: "Another startup landing page."

## Visual Identity (committed)
- **Background**: #050405 (near-black with faint violet undertone)
- **Ink**: #F5F0E8 (warm off-white)
- **Gold accent**: #F59E0B — the single committed color, used sparingly for maximum impact
- **Type**: Clash Display (display/headings) + Cabinet Grotesk (body) + JetBrains Mono (data/code)
- **Strategy**: Restrained dark with one saturated accent. Military precision.

## Anti-References
- Generic SaaS landing pages (light backgrounds, hero illustration, feature grid)
- Glassmorphism / neon cyberpunk aesthetic
- Crowdstrike / Palo Alto corporate blue
- "AI company" purple gradients
- Startup-template feel with stock security icons

## True References
- Linear.app — dark, precise, every pixel earned
- Stripe (dark mode) — information density, editorial restraint
- Palantir — serious, deep technical credibility
- Vercel — functional beauty, no waste
- Apple Mac Pro page — cinematic, confident, understated

## Design Principles
1. **Every pixel earns its place.** No decorative filler.
2. **Scroll tells the story.** Pinned scenes drive narrative, not sections.
3. **Information density over whitespace.** Pack content like a Bloomberg terminal.
4. **Motion is functional.** Camera effects reveal meaning, not entertain.
5. **Gold is sacred.** One accent color means maximum impact every use.
6. **Credibility over style.** The platform's depth is the personality.
